Moss Detection on Building Walls

Compared classical contour-based segmentation with DeepLabv3+ / ResNet-50 for detecting moss on textured wall images.

Computer visionDeepLabv3+ResNet-50Image segmentation

Engineering challenge

Detecting moss regions is difficult under varying wall textures, lighting conditions and color similarities with the background.

My contribution

Group computer-vision study. The presentation lists additional contributors; results are described as collaborative rather than exclusively mine.

Methods & implementation

  • Compared a hybrid edge/active-contour processing pipeline with a deep semantic segmentation approach.
  • Evaluated consistency on wall imagery using coverage and F-measure metrics.
  • Documented difficult cases and segmentation under/over-detection patterns.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

The reported classical pipeline averaged an F-measure of 0.831 in the presentation; the comparison also identified texture and domain-generalization limits.
Scope & interpretation: Academic group evaluation on project imagery; do not extrapolate the scores to a commercial detection product or an independent real-world benchmark.
