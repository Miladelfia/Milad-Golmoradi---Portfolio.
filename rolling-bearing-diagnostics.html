Rolling-Bearing Fault Diagnostics

Investigated rolling-bearing fault classification by combining vibration analysis, thermal imagery, signal processing and SVM techniques.

Engineering challenge

My contribution

Individual MSc thesis research, with academic supervision.

Methods & implementation
