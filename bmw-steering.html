BMW 730 Steering Dynamics

Analyzed a BMW 730 lateral model and designed active yaw-rate control with state-estimation comparisons involving Luenberger and Wiener/Kalman approaches.

MATLABState-space modelsPI controlLuenberger observerKalman filtering

Engineering challenge

Active steering requires a stable and observable lateral vehicle model and robust yaw-rate regulation under changing speed and disturbance conditions.

My contribution

Individual mathematical and simulation study based on a published vehicle model; no claim of modifying or testing a real BMW vehicle.

Methods & implementation

  • Formulated a two-state model with lateral velocity and yaw rate and evaluated stability, reachability and observability.
  • Studied steering behavior over varying speed and road-friction conditions.
  • Designed a PI yaw-rate controller and compared Luenberger and Wiener/Kalman state estimation using simulated results.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Produced documented stability and controller-response comparisons, demonstrating model-based reasoning about lateral dynamics and state estimation.
Scope & interpretation: The BMW 730 is the model used in the academic analysis; this is not a project completed for BMW or a physical vehicle test.
