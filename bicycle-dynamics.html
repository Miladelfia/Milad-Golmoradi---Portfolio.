Engineering case study / 08

Bicycle Dynamics & Feedback Control

Studied bicycle stability using dynamic models, root-locus analysis and feedback control of the Whipple bicycle model.

MATLABRoot locusState-spaceFeedback stabilization

Engineering challenge

Bicycle balance and handling depend on speed and dynamic coupling that make stability conditions nontrivial.

My contribution

Individual mathematical and simulation study.

Methods & implementation

  • Analyzed stability properties and transfer functions of bicycle dynamics.
  • Used root-locus reasoning to study self-stability and self-aligning speed.
  • Examined feedback stabilization of the Whipple bicycle representation.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Documented model-based stability and feedback comparisons for bicycle motion.
Scope & interpretation: The report is based on a mathematical vehicle model; no instrumented bicycle experiments are claimed.
