Vehicle Cruise & Adaptive Control

Built and evaluated vehicle speed and distance-control models using PI/PID feedback, anti-windup, robustness checks and observer-based control.

MATLABVehicle dynamicsPI/PID controlAnti-windupObservers

Engineering challenge

Vehicle cruise control must maintain tracking despite changes in vehicle mass, road grade and actuator saturation; adaptive cruise control also manages following distance.

My contribution

Individual simulation and controller-design study in MATLAB. Work was modeled and evaluated in software, not deployed on a physical vehicle.

Methods & implementation

  • Modeled and linearized the vehicle longitudinal dynamics around an operating condition.
  • Designed cruise-speed PI feedback and examined hill disturbances, mass variations and actuator saturation with anti-windup.
  • Studied adaptive distance control using PID and output-feedback observer-based architectures, comparing simulated responses and robustness.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Demonstrated comparative simulated speed and following-distance responses and showed how anti-windup addresses saturation-induced integrator behavior.
Scope & interpretation: Simulation-only controller design. Response plots are from the coursework reports; they do not represent road or hardware testing.
