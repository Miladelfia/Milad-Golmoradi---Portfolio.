Inverted Pendulum Stabilization

Stabilized a simulated unstable cart–pendulum system using state feedback, pole placement, LQR and integral action for cart-position tracking.

MATLABState feedbackLQRPole placementNonlinear simulation

Engineering challenge

An inverted pendulum must stay balanced while a cart transitions between position targets without destabilizing the coupled nonlinear dynamics.

My contribution

Individual controller-design and MATLAB simulation project.

Methods & implementation

  • Derived and analyzed the linearized cart–pendulum state-space system.
  • Designed feedback controllers with pole placement and LQR, then compared their simulated transients.
  • Extended the design with integral action and evaluated a simulated cart-position change from 5 m to 8 m while balancing the pendulum.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

The simulated cart approached the new reference while the pendulum recovered its upright position; the report also documents transient overshoot and actuator effort.
Scope & interpretation: Reported results are from numerical simulation. Do not interpret the control force or response graphs as measurements from a physical pendulum rig.
