Kalman Filtering for Motion Tracking

Implemented a Kalman-filter-based tracking method for noisy simulated observations of a football during penalty-kick trajectories.

MATLABKalman filteringState estimationStochastic simulation

Engineering challenge

Noisy position measurements obscure the true motion of a ball; a state estimator can combine a motion model with uncertain observations.

My contribution

Individual simulation and estimator-design study.

Methods & implementation

  • Established a state-space motion model and discrete Kalman estimation equations.
  • Simulated ten penalty-kick trajectories and noisy observations.
  • Compared estimated and reference position at the goal line under the experiment’s target accuracy threshold.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

In the reported 10 simulated shots, each goal-line estimate met the stated 0.25 m error threshold.
Scope & interpretation: These are simulated measurements and trajectories, not camera-tracking performance or real match data.
