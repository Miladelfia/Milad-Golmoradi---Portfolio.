Engineering case study / 06

Closed-Loop DC–DC Buck Converter

Modeled a buck converter and cascaded voltage/current regulation in MATLAB/Simulink across input-voltage and load variations.

MATLABSimulinkPower electronicsPI control

Engineering challenge

A regulated step-down converter must retain output-voltage control under changes in DC input and loading, including continuous and discontinuous conduction.

My contribution

Individual course project and simulation report.

Methods & implementation

  • Built the converter power-stage and feedback architecture in Simulink.
  • Implemented cascaded PI current and voltage loops with feedforward scaling.
  • Examined controller margins and transients for 140–180 V input conditions and varying loads.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Simulated regulation of a 110 V target at a 40 kHz switching frequency, with documented Bode and time-domain results.
Scope & interpretation: Simulation study; no physical PCB, converter prototype or laboratory efficiency measurement is claimed.
