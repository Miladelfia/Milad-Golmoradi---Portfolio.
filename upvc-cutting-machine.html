Engineering case study / 01

uPVC Mullion Cutting Machine

Reverse-engineered an existing industrial mullion-cutting machine, documented its mechanical structure, and recreated the components and assembly in CATIA V5.

CATIA V53D assembliesReverse engineeringMechanical drawings

Engineering challenge

Understanding and accurately documenting an existing uPVC cutting mechanism requires translating physical geometry and component interactions into a coherent digital assembly.

My contribution

Individual bachelor’s thesis project. Created the CAD model by studying an existing machine; this was not a claim to have invented or manufactured the original equipment.

Methods & implementation

  • Studied the functional arrangement and operating mechanism of an industrial uPVC mullion-cutting machine.
  • Modeled machine components and the overall assembly in CATIA V5.
  • Produced rendered views to explain the mechanism, part arrangement and machine geometry.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Delivered a complete CAD-oriented reconstruction and visual documentation of the existing machine as a reverse-engineering case study.
Scope & interpretation: Academic reverse-engineering study; the resulting 3D CAD representation was not a newly fabricated commercial machine.
