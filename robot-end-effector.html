Robotic Nail-Punching End Effector

Designed a custom aluminum holder in CATIA V5 to mount a nail-gun end effector to a robot wrist, as part of a collaborative robotics simulation project.

CATIA V5Autodesk InventorMATLABRobotics toolbox

Engineering challenge

Integrating a nail-gun tool with a robotic manipulator requires a mechanically compatible mount and an understanding of tool pose and motion.

My contribution

My direct contribution: mechanical design of the custom aluminum end-effector holder in CATIA V5. Other robot-modeling, workspace and inverse-dynamics tasks were completed collaboratively.

Methods & implementation

  • Developed the custom aluminum holder to accommodate the tool-to-wrist interface.
  • Contributed to simulation and analysis of robot motion, workspace and trajectory feasibility with the project team.
  • Used dynamic and inverse-dynamics results to discuss speed, acceleration and actuator torque in the simulated task.

Selected technical figures

Figures are sourced from the engineering reports, CAD screenshots or group project presentation. They illustrate modeling and simulation outputs, not unperformed hardware tests.

Engineering outcome

Produced a tailored CAD concept for the end-effector interface and supporting robot motion-analysis deliverables.
Scope & interpretation: University group simulation/design project; do not interpret the presented simulated robot as a physical prototype that I built.
